MDCCCCXXVIII · Corner of Fifth & Vermouth

The Gatsby
Grand

Twenty-two storeys of brass and velvet above the avenue, built the year the market still believed in itself. The lift runs on time. The orchestra tunes at eight. No one has ever been asked to leave before dawn.

Floor 2 · Accommodations

The Suites

Three addresses upstairs, each with its own temperament. Ring twice for ice.

The Fitzgerald

A corner suite for people who quote the books they haven’t finished. Champagne coupes arrive in a pyramid, unbidden, at six.

The Meridian

For the perpetually departing. A brass sextant on the writing desk, a wardrobe built for steamer trunks, and a view aimed due east.

The Aurora

The penthouse corner where the sun arrives first. Curtains open themselves at seven; the staff have learned not to ask why.

Floor 3 · After Eight O’Clock

The Ballroom

Eleven hundred crystal drops, hand-strung, and not one of them has fallen since the night the market did. The band plays until somebody wealthier than the band asks them to stop.

The chandelier has outlasted four owners, two wars, and one very determined game of indoor croquet.
