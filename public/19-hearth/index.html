Hearth

eight roofs · one stream · a sky you can turn

a village kept by hand

Somewhere the evenings
still arrive on time.

Down past the millpond, past the last cold lamp on the ridge road, sits a village that hasn't hurried in three hundred years. The bakery breathes smoke before the sun clears the hill. The windmill turns for no one in particular. And every evening, right on schedule, the sky changes its mind — go on, turn it yourself.

Day Night

Hover any roof to meet its household. Nudge the switch and the whole valley follows — walls, windows, and somewhere in the grass, crickets you'll have to imagine.

The Village Registry

Eight addresses, kept current by whoever's awake. Hover a roof above — or tab to it — and it'll introduce itself.

The Flour & Ember Bakery

First smoke of the morning, last light of the night. Opens at dawn, closes when the ovens say so.

Starwell Observatory

A dome that only opens after moonrise. Bring your own coat — the stars don't heat the place.

The Quiet Chapter

Six shelves, one armchair, no overdue fines. Always one lamp lit for whoever's still reading.

Miller’s Rest

Turns the valley's grain — and, most days, just turns. Nobody's checked the gears in years.

Birch Hollow Cottage

Smoke by supper time, whether or not there's anyone home. The chimney keeps its own hours.

Moss End Cottage

The ivy arrived before the family did. Neither one shows signs of leaving.

Amber Gate Cottage

Porch light never sleeps. Neither, some nights, does the kettle.

The Well House

Coldest water in the valley. Wish-granting unconfirmed, but nobody's stopped trying.
