Manifesto

There is a moment before the garment — before the seam, before the first mark on cloth — when nothing has been decided yet. Collection Nº14 lives inside that moment, and refuses to leave it.

Absence

Absence.

We removed what a dress is expected to say. What is left is weight, fall, the quiet architecture of a body in a room. No print. No slogan. No proof of labor left on the surface. Six looks, one discipline — wool, silk, void.

Nothing performs here. Everything is worn the way a room holds its silence after the door closes.

Bodoni Moda, Figtree. Two tones, nothing else.
