Les Compositions

Three hours of light,
held still

Each Aurelia is a single hour of the day, caught at its point of turning — then suspended, indefinitely. We make three. There is no fourth hour worth keeping.

L’Architecture

Anatomy of
an accord

We compose in three registers, the way dawn arrives: an instant, an hour, a memory.

  • Tête
    The instant

    Bergamote, aldéhydes, poire givrée. It vanishes in twenty minutes; we spend two years on it.

  • Cœur
    The hour

    Rose de mai, iris pallida, fleur d’oranger. The temperature of skin, the exact middle of the day.

  • Fond
    The memory

    Santal, musc blanc, ambre gris. What the room remembers after you have gone.

La Maison

We do not bottle flowers.

We bottle the hour they opened.

Aurelia was founded in Grasse in 1947, in a house with seventeen windows and no electric light. Élise Aurel composed only between five and seven in the morning, when the air was still cold enough to hold one scent apart from another.

We keep her hours. Three compositions, never more; each touched only when its hour changes — which is to say, never quickly. The rest is patience, silk, and weather.

É. Aurel
