# PASSES — 11 · KOWLOON NIGHTS

## Build — 2026-07-12

**Concept.** A neon cyberpunk night-market street — rain on asphalt, humming
signs, Wong Kar-wai glow. Signature technique: a full isometric street built in
pure CSS 3D (no images, no WebGL), with canvas rain layered over it.

**Implemented.**
- CSS 3D world: 1600×1600 ground plane under `rotateX(60deg) rotateZ(-45deg)`,
  `preserve-3d` throughout. Twelve buildings (six far towers, six near stalls),
  each three faces — roof at `translateZ(h)`, walls stood up via
  `rotateX(90deg)` / `rotateZ(90deg) rotateX(90deg)` with ground-edge origins.
- Window grids generated by a seeded PRNG (stable street per load); ~14% of lit
  cells flicker on `steps(1,end)` tracks with negative random delays.
- Neon signs billboarded with the inverse-camera transform
  `rotateZ(45deg) rotateX(-60deg)` at true 3D coordinates; bilingual glyphs
  (麺 酒 電 茶 藥 占卜) in Noto Sans JP; hum + erratic dying-letter keyframes
  (masthead N, Blue Orchid R).
- Canvas rain: 150 depth-graded slanted strokes, per-drop landing floors,
  elliptical splash rings; DPR capped at 2; paused on `document.hidden` and
  when hero leaves viewport (IntersectionObserver).
- Puddles + per-sign reflection streaks as `mix-blend-mode: screen` gradients
  flat on the ground plane; steam blob loop; SVG cables with lantern dots;
  SVG neon-tube noodle-bowl sign in the directory.
- Camera: cursor parallax (lerped) + ambient sinusoidal drift so recordings
  are always alive.
- Below fold: six-stall directory with ignite-on-hover neon cards, LED marquee
  ticker, noir-poetic bilingual copy, footer per spec.
- Guide page in-aesthetic; reduced-motion path (static rain frame, parked
  camera, flickers locked on); focus-visible styles; meta/OG/favicon.

## Pass 1 — 2026-07-18 (art direction)

Fresh-eyes review against the screenshots turned up a genuine rendering bug
plus several composition gaps; fixed the bug and used the room it freed to
rebalance the hero.

- **Fixed sign occlusion / z-fighting.** Three signs — GOLDEN BOWL, OK
  ELECTRIC and the 占卜 (fortune-teller) tag — were rendering with a diagonal
  dark wedge sliced through their text (a classic Chromium artifact from
  nested `preserve-3d` contexts: each `.bld` is its own 3D group sorted as a
  unit against sibling signs, so a roof polygon can mis-sort in front of a
  sign that's actually nearer camera). Text was genuinely illegible in the
  screenshots ("K碗麺家", "TRIC" instead of "OK ELECTRIC"). Fixed by pushing
  the affected signs further toward the street (larger `y`, i.e. more
  foreground) instead of just taller, which clears the offending wall/roof
  plane without pushing the sign off the top of the viewport. Verified pixel
  by pixel with cropped zooms before/after.
- **Recovered two dead signs.** The small "BLUE ORCHID" tag (home of the
  brief's dying-letter buzz) and the "MIDNIGHT LEAF" tag were positioned
  directly behind the masthead's text scrim — present in the DOM, animating,
  completely invisible in every screenshot. Removed the redundant Blue Orchid
  tag (the vertical 蘭花酒吧 sign already carries the brand, and the dying
  letter lives loudly on the "KOWLOON" masthead itself), and relocated
  Midnight Leaf into open street real estate on the right side, giving it its
  own dying-letter flicker so the technique still reads on a second sign.
  This also fills what was dead black space in the upper-right of the frame.
- **Hard cut between hero and directory.** The ticker bar dropped straight
  into flat black with no bridge. Added a seam treatment on `.directory`
  (radial pink/cyan bleed + a thin hairline) so the transition reads as
  continued neon glow rather than a template-style section break.
  Tightened the directory's top padding slightly to match.
- **Generic footer.** Restyled to match the site's own UI language instead
  of default gray caption text: a pulsing status dot (echoing the "MARKET
  OPEN" indicator), the stall number in sign-yellow, the site name in neon
  pink, and a matching hairline rule at the seam.

Verified with `tools/snap.mjs` at both viewports after every change
(desktop/desktop-late/mobile) plus full-page captures for the directory and
footer; zero console/page/request errors throughout.
