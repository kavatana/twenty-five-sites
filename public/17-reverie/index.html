Reverie — open only while you sleep

A hotel with no address,
reachable only by closing your eyes.

Through that door: a lobby, a staircase that never lands, and a room already made up in your name. Scroll gently — everything here is still deciding what it looks like.

keep drifting ↓

A note left on the pillow — Rule one: the moon here is shy, it only moves when you do. Rule two: nothing casts its shadow where you'd expect. Rule three: you are not required to remember any of this by morning.

Lost & found, third floor

Some things never made it to a room.

A key to Room 000

Recovered from the stairwell for the fourth time this week. It fits every lock in the building and opens nothing.

One chair, reserved

Held for a guest who checked in decades before the hotel was built. Housekeeping still dusts it nightly.

An umbrella, still dry

Kept at the door for the rain that falls upward in the east wing. No one has asked for it. Yet.

Sub-level, no floor number

The stairs that only go down.

Something has been descending since before you arrived — a small light, unhurried, never reaching a landing that holds it. Housekeeping has stopped asking where it's headed.
